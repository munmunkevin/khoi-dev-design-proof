Monday to Friday, on one board

A week with team Little Sprouts

Hanh runs a 6-person preschool team. Minh coordinates volunteer shifts at the food bank. Lan Anh owns a small flower shop. Minh and Lan Anh are both parents at the school, volunteering on the parents' committee — so the school board is their board too. They share one board — here is a week on it.

✓ Monday 11 May — Hanh opens the week

Hanh writes Monday's lesson plan at the top of the board: four tasks, three owners, one Friday deadline. Everyone can see the whole week before the first class starts.

✓ Wednesday 13 May — Minh counts the slips

By Wednesday, 47 permission slips are back for the monthly parents' meeting, and the paper-and-glue order Minh placed on Tuesday has arrived. Two things the team no longer has to chase.

✓ Friday 15 May — Lan Anh closes the week

Lan Anh photographs the reading corner and ticks it done. The team looks back at the board: 2 of 4 tasks finished, 2 carried into next week — and the finished list, dates and all, is right there to show for it.
